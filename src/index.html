Daily check-in

A short, private check-in about how you are today. It is not a clinical questionnaire and it does not diagnose anything. It never raises alerts and never earns points.

The days before today are simulated for the demo. What you answer today is saved only on this device.

Who sees this

  • You: everything you answer.
  • Rest of the crew: nothing, ever. No group averages either.
  • Crew medical officer: nothing, unless you turn on sharing below.
  • Flight surgeons on Earth: nothing in this prototype. Health and psychological communications are private under NASA standards.
